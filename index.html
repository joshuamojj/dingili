Original animated nursery rhymes for young children.

Dingili is a small independent studio run by a single creator. We write our own lyrics, compose our own songs and animate every video ourselves, then publish them on our two YouTube channels. All of our videos are made for children and are marked as Made for Kids on YouTube.

About our use of YouTube API Services

We run a small private publishing tool that uploads our own finished videos to our own two YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services and is operated only by the channel owner. It has no public interface, no other users and no sign-up: nobody other than the owner can use it or connect an account to it.

By using our tool we are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Our own practices are set out in our Privacy Policy.

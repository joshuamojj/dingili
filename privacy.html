Privacy Policy

Dingili · دینگیلی

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Dingili ("we", "our") publishes original animated nursery rhymes on the YouTube channels @dingili and @dingilikids. This policy explains what data our publishing tool handles.

Our publishing tool is private

We operate one internal tool that uploads our own finished videos to our own two YouTube channels. It runs as a scheduled server-side script. It has no user interface, no accounts and no sign-up. It is used by the channel owner only. No other person can connect a Google Account to it, and we do not offer it to anyone else.

What we collect from you

Nothing. This website collects no personal information. It has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not set cookies. Our publishing tool does not collect, request, store or process personal data belonging to any other person.

Data our tool uses

To upload to our own channels, the tool is authorised with the channel owner's own Google Account through Google OAuth 2.0. It stores the resulting access credentials as encrypted repository secrets so that uploads can run automatically. It requests only the access it needs to upload and manage our own videos, and it reads no information about viewers or any other YouTube user.

The video files, titles, descriptions and thumbnails it sends to YouTube are our own content. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we use no data for advertising or profiling.

YouTube API Services

Our tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it we are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's own handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy, which we encourage you to read.

Revoking access

The Google Account used by our tool can revoke its access at any time from the Google security settings page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once access is revoked the tool can no longer upload anything.

Retention and deletion

The stored credentials are kept only for as long as we keep publishing to these channels, and are deleted when they are no longer needed or when access is revoked. Because we hold no personal data about any other person, there is no other data to request, export or delete. If you believe we hold data about you, write to us and we will respond.

Children

Our videos are made for children and are marked as Made for Kids on YouTube, but we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, children included. Viewing happens on YouTube, under Google's own policies, not on this website.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and change the date above.
